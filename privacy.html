Privacy Policy

OutreachPilot · Last updated 29 October 2026

In short: OutreachPilot is a desktop application that runs on your computer. It has no server and no developer-run backend. The Google data it accesses is processed on your device and is never sent to the developer or to any third party.

Summary of data handling

  • App name: OutreachPilot (shown as "outreachpilot" on Google's consent screen). Home page: fraktosh.github.io/outreach-pilot.
  • What the app accesses from your Google Account: your email address; the ability to send email on your behalf; the recipient and date headers of your Sent mail; and the sender of recent inbox messages (plus the text only of replies from people you emailed and of delivery-failure notices) to detect replies and bounces.
  • What the app does not access: message bodies of your Sent mail, other inbox messages, drafts, contacts, calendar, Drive or any other Google data. It never modifies, moves, labels or deletes your mail.
  • Where data goes: to Google (to send the mail you approved, over HTTPS) and nowhere else. No data is sent to the developer or to third parties.
  • How long it is kept: only on your own computer, until you sign out or delete the local folder.
  • Security: the sign-in token is stored in a file readable only by your user account, and all communication with Google uses HTTPS.

How we protect your data

  • No Google user data leaves your computer except the mail you chose to send, which goes to Google over HTTPS (TLS).
  • The developer runs no server, database or analytics, so there is nothing to breach on our side and no copy of your data to leak.
  • OAuth uses the installed-app flow with PKCE and a one-time loopback redirect to 127.0.0.1. The app never sees or stores your Google password.
  • The token file is created with owner-only permissions where the operating system supports it. Anyone with access to your operating-system account could read it, so keep your computer account protected.
  • The app requests the narrowest set of permissions it needs and does not use the full-access https://mail.google.com/ scope.

Deleting your data and withdrawing consent

Because the data lives on your own device, you are in control of it. Choose "Sign out" in the app, remove OutreachPilot at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the .outreach_pilot folder in your home directory to erase everything the app stored. The developer holds no copy, so there is no data on our side to delete. For any question or request, write to sahasoham814@gmail.com.

2. Google user data we access

If you choose "Sign in with Google", the app requests these permissions through Google's OAuth 2.0 consent screen:

  • Your email address and sign-in identity (openid, email): to show which account is signed in and to use it as the sender address.
  • Send email on your behalf (gmail.send): to send the messages that you reviewed and approved.

The app does not request permission to read, list, search, modify or delete your mail, and it cannot do so. It sees only the messages it sends.

3. How the data is used and stored

  • All processing happens locally on your computer. We do not operate any server that receives your Google data.
  • The app stores a revocable OAuth refresh token and your email address in ~/.outreach_pilot/oauth_token.json on your device, with owner-only file permissions where the operating system supports it.
  • Recipient addresses and send status (for example "sent", "failed") are kept in a local database in the same folder. This is the campaign history you see in the app.
  • Message content you approve is passed to Gmail only to send it.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, share, transfer or disclose Google user data to anyone, and it is not used to train any AI or machine-learning model. The app has no access to your Gmail content beyond the messages it sends. The language-model providers the app can use for drafting only receive the CV and the public information about the recipient that you supply for drafting.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

OutreachPilot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • We use Google user data only to provide and improve the user-facing features described above.
  • We do not transfer the data to others except as needed to provide those features, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or sale with your prior consent.
  • We do not use the data for serving advertisements.
  • No human reads your Google user data. Because the app runs only on your device, the developer has no access to it.

6. Your controls

  • Sign out: choose "Sign out" in the app. It revokes the token with Google and deletes the local token file.
  • Revoke from Google: remove OutreachPilot at myaccount.google.com/permissions at any time.
  • Delete local data: delete the .outreach_pilot folder in your home directory. This removes the token, the campaign database and settings.

7. Retention

Data stays on your device until you sign out or delete the folder above. Google access tokens expire after about an hour; the refresh token is kept until you sign out, revoke access, or Google expires it.

8. Other data

To draft emails the app sends your CV text and public information about each recipient to the language-model provider you configure (Google Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic), using your own API key. That use is governed by the provider's terms. To find and verify addresses the app also makes ordinary web and DNS requests and may open a connection to a recipient's mail server to check that an address exists, without sending any message.

9. Children

OutreachPilot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
